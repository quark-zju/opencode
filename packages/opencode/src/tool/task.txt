Delegate a bounded, multistep task to a specialized agent. Use direct tools for simple file reads or searches.

- Choose a listed `subagent_type`. A new call starts with fresh context; set `task_id` only to resume a previous task.
- State the goal, scope, whether to research or edit, expected result, and useful verification in the prompt.
- Do not duplicate delegated work. Continue with non-overlapping work while background tasks run.
- Launch independent tasks together when useful. Treat their results as authoritative unless they conflict with other evidence.
- Agent results are not shown to the user; summarize relevant findings in your response.
