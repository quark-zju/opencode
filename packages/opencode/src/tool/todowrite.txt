Track progress for non-trivial work with multiple steps. Skip it for simple or purely informational requests.

- Keep exactly one item `in_progress` while work remains.
- Update statuses as work advances; do not batch updates at the end.
- Mark an item `completed` only after its work and required verification are done.
- Keep blocked or partial work `in_progress` and add the blocker as a follow-up item.
- Preserve user-provided commands verbatim and keep items specific and actionable.
